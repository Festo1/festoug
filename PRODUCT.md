# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Freelance clients — businesses (local and international) evaluating Festo Wampamba for full-stack web development, server administration, and network/IT infrastructure work. They arrive from search, referrals, or social links, usually on a first visit, deciding within minutes whether this person is worth contacting. Secondary: recruiters and fellow developers browsing the portfolio and blog.

## Product Purpose

festoug.com is a personal platform: portfolio + services showcase + digital store + blog, with an admin CMS behind it. Its job is to convert a first-time visitor into a client inquiry (Get Started / Contact) by proving, through the site's own craft, that Festo ships polished, reliable product. The site itself is the strongest work sample.

## Brand Personality

Precise, calm, dependable. Quiet confidence over flash: engineered motion, tight spacing, restrained color. The dark violetDark identity (charcoal base, violet accent, Archivo/Space Grotesk display) executed at Apple/Linear craft level. Copy in first person, direct and warm but never salesy.

## Anti-references

- The unmodified shadcn/template look — stock tokens, system fonts, generic cards.
- Mismatched sub-surfaces (e.g. auth pages in a different palette than the site).
- Self-rated skill percentage bars and other credibility-undermining portfolio clichés.
- Loud SaaS landing-page grammar: gradient text, hero metrics, identical icon-card grids, eyebrow labels above every section.
- Slow, unstyled third-party embeds that break the dark canvas (blank map boxes, layout-shifting widgets).

## Design Principles

1. **The site is the work sample.** Every detail — loading states, motion easing, empty states — is evidence of how Festo builds for clients. Practice what you preach.
2. **Motion is engineered, not decorated.** Purposeful, exponential-ease-out reveals that fit what they reveal; no uniform fade-on-scroll reflex; reduced-motion always honored.
3. **One identity everywhere.** violetDark tokens govern every surface — main pages, auth, store, dashboard, admin. Blue never sneaks in.
4. **Show real proof, not claims.** Real projects (GitHub-sourced), real testimonials, concrete services. Replace self-ratings with verifiable substance.
5. **Calm conversion.** Every page offers a quiet, obvious next step toward Get Started/Contact without shouting.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA. Body text ≥4.5:1 contrast in both themes (light mode included — it stays and gets fixed). Full keyboard navigation with visible focus. Every animation has a `prefers-reduced-motion` alternative. Touch targets ≥44px. Color never the sole indicator.
